# About Expedient Employment

## GitHub About box (short description)

## Project description (social preview / long form)

Expedient Employment is a local-first job-search assistant that turns the scattered hunt for a new role into one reproducible pipeline. It searches LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and ZipRecruiter, confirms every posting is real and still open on the employer's own site, and scores each role against your resume with a transparent, five-part rubric that shows its evidence and gaps. A small team of AI agents — a recruiter, an independent verifier, and an application assistant — is coordinated by a Paperclip control plane, and nothing is ever submitted without your explicit per-role approval. An Electron desktop app wraps the whole workflow with a dashboard, live search, jobs shortlist, per-site login browser tabs, and settings editors. Everything runs on your own machine: job data, scores, reports, and your resume stay local.

## Highlights

- 🔎 Multi-board job discovery (JobSpy) with automatic WebClaw fallback
- ✅ Employer-page verification before any scoring happens
- 📊 Deterministic, explainable match scoring with evidence and gaps; optional AI blend and self-hosted Resume-Matcher ATS evidence
- 🤖 Three-agent Paperclip team: recruiter → verifier → approval-gated application assistant
- 🖥️ Electron GUI: dashboard with pipeline doctor, service health, search, jobs, agents, browser login tabs, and settings editors
- 🔒 Local-first privacy: resume redacted in memory; no submission without explicit human confirmation
- 🪟 Windows installer + portable zip; macOS/Linux build targets included
